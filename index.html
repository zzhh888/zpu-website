Biography

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Pharmacognosy, School of Pharmacy, Naval Medical University. My research focuses on interactions between medicinal plants and associated microorganisms, particularly the ecological and functional roles of plant-associated microbial communities.

My academic training spans Pharmacognosy and Chinese Materia Medica, complemented by professional experience in a hospital pharmacy department. I am interested in integrating pharmacognostic, microbiological, and analytical approaches to investigate medicinal plant resources, plant-associated microbiomes, and bioactive natural products.

Research Interests
  • Interactions between medicinal plants and associated microorganisms
  • Pharmacognosy
  • Plant-associated microbiomes
  • Medicinal plant resources and quality evaluation
  • Bioactive natural products from medicinal plants and associated microorganisms
